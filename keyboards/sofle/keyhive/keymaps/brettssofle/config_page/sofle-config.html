Keeps the computer awake: after this long without any key, knob or trackpad use, the pointer moves 1 pixel and straight back (again every interval while you're away). Applies right away and switches off whenever the keyboard is unplugged. The Mouse wiggle key (Special) toggles it too.

Live Z

Z is the trackpad's signal strength (0–63). A press clicks when Z reaches the threshold and lets go below the release Z (or when you lift). Set the threshold between your light-touch peaks and your firm-press peaks.

The OLED pet

Settings

Pet
Name
Needs care
Can die

Choose who hatches from the new egg. It starts as an egg (about 10 minutes), is a baby until a day old, a child until three days, then an adult. Good care keeps it growing at full speed.

On the keyboard: press the right knob until the pet fills the right screen. There, turn the right knob to choose Feed / Play / Sleep / Clean, tap it to do it, and hold it about a second for the next screen. Typing counts as play, so normal use keeps the pet happy; it only gets hungry or sad, never dies, unless Can die is on.
