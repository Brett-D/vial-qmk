Network mode

Typing the address

On the NET screen, a click of the right knob types the address into the address bar you have selected; with this on (the default) it also presses Enter, so the page opens. That Enter never answers the page's CONF? — confirm with the knob. Saved on the keyboard.

Screen

Also on the keyboard: on the INFO page, turn the right knob for the Display menu (Brite, Sleep, Off, Back; tap to choose, turn to change, tap again to save). While the screen is off, a knob press wakes it. The config, CONF? and network screens always show. Lower brightness and a short sleep help the OLED last (burn-in). Saved on the keyboard.

Responsiveness

How long each pass of the keyboard's main loop takes, on each half (the longest over the last 10 s, and the worst since the last reset). A key is read once per pass, so the longest pass is about the longest a press can wait to be seen, plus the 5 ms debounce; keys on the right half also wait for the left half's next pass. If you notice lag, press Measure, keep using the keyboard until it happens, then Copy and send the numbers.

Live Z

Z is the trackpad's signal strength (0–63). A press clicks when Z reaches the threshold and lets go below the release Z (or when you lift). Set the threshold between your light-touch peaks and your firm-press peaks.

A reaction lights up for a few seconds over your lighting, in the key-press brightness, and never changes the lighting settings. None during config mode, LED calibration or typed delivery (the sad and sleepy ones also not while this page is connected).

Choose who hatches from the new egg. It starts as an egg (about 10 minutes), is a baby until a day old, a child until three days, then an adult. Good care keeps it growing at full speed.

On the keyboard: press the right knob until the pet fills the right screen. There, turn the right knob to choose Feed / Play / Sleep / Clean, tap it to do it, and hold it about a second for the next screen. Typing counts as play and gives it a little energy (up to 80; sleep fills the rest), so normal use keeps the pet happy; it only gets hungry or sad, never dies, unless Can die is on.

My macros

A macro types a sequence of keys or text with one key. Make it here, then put it on a key: Layout tab → click a key → My macros. Ready-made ones (Delete line, Duplicate line, Lock PC…) are in the picker's Macros group and need no setup. Saved on the keyboard (Vial's macros M0–M15; the names too).
